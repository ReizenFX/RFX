Setup Required

To extract audio natively, After Effects needs a custom output template. Follow these easy steps once:

  1. In After Effects, go to the top menu:
    Edit > Templates > Output Module...
  2. Click New to create a new template.
  3. Name the template exactly: WAV
  4. Click Edit...
  5. Change Format to Waveform Audio.
  6. Click OK on both windows.
